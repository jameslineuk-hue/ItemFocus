ItemFocus

Label laptops, keys, wallets, or anything important with a QR tag. Someone who finds the item opens the link and sees how to reach you—without broadcasting your details to the whole internet.

  • Each tag has a unique code (for example IF-AB12CD).
  • Only people who scan or type the full link can see your contact card.
  • Profiles are stored in Supabase; this site is a small Python API plus static pages on Render.
